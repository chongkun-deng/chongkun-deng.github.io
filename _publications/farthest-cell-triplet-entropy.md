**Status:** Preprint  
**Author:** Chongkun Deng  
**arXiv:** [2609.02362](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02362)

[View abstract on arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02362) \| [Download PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.02362)

This paper introduces **farthest-cell triplet entropy**, a comparison-based statistic that measures the predictability of the farthest prototype among random triples. It requires only ordinal comparisons - not coordinates or numerical distances - and is invariant under monotone transformations of the underlying dissimilarities.

I derive high-dimensional limit laws for isotropic Euclidean and hyperbolic radial models, showing how shell variation, dimension, and hyperbolic curvature interact through a common entropy master curve. Simulations support the theory and demonstrate scale-invariant curvature estimation under calibrated isotropic models. The results also clarify the method's limitations: curvature recovery is model-calibrated, requires an external length unit for absolute curvature, and is not robust to angular anisotropy.
