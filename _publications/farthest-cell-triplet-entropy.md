**Author:** Chongkun Deng  
**arXiv:** [2609.02362](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02362)

[View abstract on arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02362) \| [Download PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.02362)

This paper introduces **farthest-cell triplet entropy**, the conditional Shannon entropy of a query's farthest-prototype label given a random triple of prototypes, averaged over triples. Estimation of the statistic uses the farthest labels and is invariant under a common strictly increasing transformation of the dissimilarities.

For isotropic radial models, I derive high-dimensional limits governed by the competition between angular fluctuations and radial variation. In hyperbolic space, curvature changes the effective shell parameter through an explicit amplification factor. The same limiting entropy curve connects the Euclidean and hyperbolic models.

The paper also examines model-calibrated curvature inference through simulations and an anisotropy stress test. Inversion targets a scale-invariant curvature quantity and depends on radial calibration, isotropy, and a verified monotone operating interval. Absolute curvature requires an external length unit. These restrictions motivate further statistical questions about estimation uncertainty and sensitivity to model assumptions.
