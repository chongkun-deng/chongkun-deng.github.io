**Status:** Preprint  
**Author:** Chongkun Deng

This paper introduces **farthest-cell triplet entropy**, a comparison-based statistic that measures the predictability of the farthest prototype among random triples. It requires only ordinal comparisons - not coordinates or numerical distances - and is invariant under monotone transformations of the underlying dissimilarities.

I derive high-dimensional limit laws for isotropic Euclidean and hyperbolic models, showing how shell variation, dimension, and hyperbolic curvature interact through a common entropy master curve. Simulations support the theory, demonstrate scale-invariant curvature estimation under calibrated isotropic models, and identify anisotropy as an important limitation.
